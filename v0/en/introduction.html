    • Introduction¶

    vrpRouting is a PostgreSQL extension that extends of pgRouting extension, by adding solutions to several Vehicle Routing Problems.

    The project is supported and maintained by

    • Paragon Corporation

    • erosion

    and a broad user community.

    vrpRouting is a sub product of pgRouting organization organization.
